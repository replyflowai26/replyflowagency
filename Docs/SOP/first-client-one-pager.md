# Inbound Lead Capture — one page (client-facing draft)

> A draft a human reviews and adapts before sending. **Do not send, publish or
> promise anything not listed as included.** No testimonials, prices or results
> are claimed here; a human adds their own numbers.

## The problem

Leads arrive through a form, an ad, or a DM — and someone has to *notice* them
manually. The good ones get answered late, and a late first reply loses the
deal. Nothing was broken in the lead; it just arrived where nobody was watching.

## The outcome we sell

**Every inbound lead is captured, scored against your own checklist, and the
right person is alerted within seconds — with a human approving anything that
goes back out.**

We measure speed and capture rate, not revenue:

- Lead arrives → owner alerted in **under 60 seconds**.
- **100%** of captured leads reach your pipeline — none silently dropped.
- **Zero** messages sent without your approval.

## What's included (one channel, one destination)

For a single inbound channel (one website form **or** one webhook endpoint):

1. **Capture** — the lead payload is accepted and validated; junk is rejected.
2. **Qualify** — scored against a short checklist *you* define (deterministic,
   explainable, the same every time).
3. **Record** — a durable entry with a full event timeline you can inspect.
4. **Alert + approve** — the owner is notified; high-value leads are held until
   an owner approves. Approval and rejection happen exactly once.
5. **Deliver** — the lead is delivered to the one agreed destination with a
   signed, idempotent request.
6. **See it** — a dashboard run view showing status, timeline and any failure,
   with a clear reason rather than a silent error.

## What's deliberately not included

Unattended or AI-authored sending, multi-step follow-up sequences, reply
detection and opt-out handling, multi-channel campaigns (voice/WhatsApp), a CRM
rebuild, and any guarantee of revenue or lead volume. We keep the first step
small and trustworthy; the follow-on scope is scoped separately after this works.

## The pilot (2 weeks, one channel)

- **Prerequisites from you:** one inbound channel and its destination, a short
  qualification checklist, and the name of the person who approves hot leads.
- **Timeline:** week 1 configure + dry-run against a staging endpoint; week 2
  live on your channel; end of week 2 review.
- **Acceptance:** a test lead arrives in under 60 seconds; a hot lead holds until
  approved and then delivers exactly once; a duplicate never creates a second
  record; every failure shows a clear reason.
- **Support boundary during the pilot:** we own configuration, delivery and the
  failure reasons. You own the inbound channel and the approver's response time.
