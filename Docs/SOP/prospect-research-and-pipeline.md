## 1. Research procedure (reproducible)

1. Pick one geography + 2–3 niches where inbound form leads are visible and a
   slow first reply costs money (home services, dental, roofing, legal, med-spa).
2. Search for the **estimate / appointment / contact form page** specifically —
   not the homepage. A dedicated form page is the strongest public signal that
   leads arrive through a form.
3. Record only: company name, public website, the form URL, the niche, and a
   **verbatim evidence snippet** from the page (the response-time promise).
4. Do **not** harvest emails or phone numbers into this document. The outreach
   route is the public form (or a LinkedIn/company contact the prospect
   publishes themselves).
5. Mark `fit` using the rubric below, and set `status = new`.

## 2. Scoring criteria (evidence-based, visible signals only)

| Signal | Points | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page states a **response-time promise** ("within 1 business hour", "same day", "within 24 hours") | **+30** | They sell speed → a manual process is the thing at risk. Strongest signal. |
| Dedicated **estimate / appointment form** page (not just a phone number) | **+25** | Leads demonstrably arrive through a form we can capture. |
| States **emergency / after-hours** availability | **+15** | Mixed, out-of-hours lead arrival — exactly where manual notice fails. |
| **Single / few local locations** (public address evidence) | **+15** | Fits the 2–25 person ICP; no procurement cycle. |
| Mentions **"new patients / free estimate / no-obligation"** | **+10** | High-intent inbound, worth a fast reply. |
| No stated response promise, phone-first only | **0** | Still callable, weaker evidence. |

**Threshold:** pursue at **≥ 70**; nurture 50–69; skip below 50.
**Hard disqualifier (never score):** franchise/call-centre, national brand, or a
page that already advertises an automated chatbot reply.
