REPLYFLOW-AI SETUP

STEP 1:
Extract the ZIP.

STEP 2:
Open ReplyFlow-AI-Final.

STEP 3:
Keep all client projects inside Projects/Active.

STEP 4:
Each new service starts from Services/.
Duplicate an existing service folder when creating a new AI product.

STEP 5:
Store workflows in Workflows/Production.
Never edit Production directly. Test inside Workflows/Testing first.

STEP 6:
Keep SOPs, pricing, deployment docs and roadmap inside Docs.

STEP 7:
Store logos, PDFs, videos and images inside Assets.

STEP 8:
Maintain branding inside Branding.

This structure is designed so your agency can scale from 1 client to 100+ clients without reorganizing files.
